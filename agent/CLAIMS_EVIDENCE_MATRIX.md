Status vocabulary is defined in `../AGENTS.md`. Area/capacity-equivalent is the
main analysis. Count results remain denominator evidence and sensitivity
comparators. A claim may enter the abstract only when its primary evidence is
`REPRODUCED` and its uncertainty/validation gate is either passed or stated in
the same sentence.

## Abstract gate

- C0 may enter after rechecking the frozen index at build time.
- C1--C4 may enter with the separate-denominator, first-event attribution and
  target-population scope stated compactly.
- C5--C7 may enter the main Results with the reproduced C8 intervals. Any
  abstract statement must distinguish the supported count/area-summed
  observed-city aggregate from heterogeneous city and transition directions.
- C9 does not block reporting area or capacity-equivalent scenarios, but it
  blocks any measured-nameplate wording.
- C10 may support why event counts and area outcomes diverge; it is not a
  general inequality claim beyond strict-event hosts.
- C14 may enter the main Results with C8 interval support, but the abstract may
  not turn 13 supported city directions into a universal city-level law; the
  coarsest cluster sensitivity also makes Boston's interval cross one.
- C11 stays outside the main Results until its join/model gate passes. C12 has
  a reproduced partial-validation bundle, but candidate-domain and
  anchor-survivor selection remain explicit scope limitations. C13 is excluded
  from the current paper.

**Changing the denominator changes the city-level comparison.** Cities are ordered from highest to lowest by the ratio of PV first appearances per building observation, with the pooled row last. Open circles show PV first appearances per building observation, diamonds show PV area per building observation, and filled squares show PV area per square metre of observed plan-view roof mask. For existing buildings, building and roof quantities are summed across eligible observed cohort transitions. The vertical line marks equality; connections compare descriptions within a city and are not time trends. Only roof-area-normalized values are labelled. All values are descriptive full-AOI point estimates without uncertainty intervals.

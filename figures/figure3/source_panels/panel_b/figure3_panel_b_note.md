# Figure 3b paired boxen note

Each city row shows the distribution of anchor PV union area across unique host buildings with a strict first observed PV appearance. The upper orange lane is the newly observed route and the lower green lane is the existing route. Nested bands show the 1st–99th, 10th–90th, and 25th–75th percentile ranges. A vertical line marks the median and an open diamond marks the mean. Band height does not encode the number of hosts. These are distribution summaries, not uncertainty intervals.

Cities use Panel a's descending PV first-appearance-ratio order, and the pooled row concatenates city host records without duplicating them. The three lower rows report pooled pathway means; temporal conflict is QA only. The upper scale applies a nominal 0.20 kWdc per square metre conversion and is not measured capacity.

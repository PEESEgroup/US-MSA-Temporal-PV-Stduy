**PV-area distributions differ between newly observed and existing building hosts.** Paired horizontal distribution bands show host-level anchor PV union area for newly observed buildings (upper orange lanes) and existing buildings (lower green lanes). Nested bands mark the 1st–99th, 10th–90th, and 25th–75th percentile ranges; vertical lines mark medians and open diamonds mark means. Band height does not represent host count, and the bands are distribution summaries rather than uncertainty intervals. Cities follow Panel a's descending PV first-appearance-ratio order, with the pooled row last. The three lower rows show pooled pathway means, with temporal conflict retained only for QA. The upper scale is a nominal 0.20 kWdc m⁻² translation, not measured capacity.

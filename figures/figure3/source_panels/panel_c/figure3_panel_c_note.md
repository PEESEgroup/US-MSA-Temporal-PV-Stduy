# Figure 3c paired-column concentration draft note

Each city occupies one aligned row in both columns. The left column shows the share of route-level PV area carried by the largest 1% of unique strict-event hosts; the right column shows the Gini coefficient of host-level anchor PV union area. Terracotta squares denote newly observed Buildings, green circles denote existing Buildings, and thin lines pair the two routes within a city rather than showing time or uncertainty. The pooled row concatenates city host records and is separated from the city rows.

Small `n=` labels identify newly observed-Building routes for which the top-1% group contains only one or two hosts. These labels qualify thin-tail leverage; they are not inferential uncertainty estimates. Both concentration quantities attribute all anchor PV union area on a host to its first strict adjacent PV appearance. They do not measure citywide adoption, installed nameplate capacity, or a causal effect.

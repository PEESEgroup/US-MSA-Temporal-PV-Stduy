**Broad inequality and extreme-tail concentration distinguish different features of host-level PV area.** For each city, the left column gives the share of route-level anchor PV union area carried by the largest 1% of unique strict-event hosts, while the right column gives the corresponding Gini coefficient. Squares denote newly observed Buildings and circles denote existing Buildings; thin lines pair routes within a city and are not time trends or uncertainty intervals. Small `n=` labels mark newly observed-Building routes in which the largest 1% comprises only one or two hosts. The separated pooled row concatenates host records across cities. All quantities are descriptive full-AOI distribution summaries based on area attributed to each host's first strict adjacent PV appearance.

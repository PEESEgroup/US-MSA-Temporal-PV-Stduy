# Figure 1c lag iteration

Each city curve shows the non-cumulative PV union-area share in each one-year lag bin. Every bin uses all resolved PV area in that city as its denominator, while only relationships with interval-resolved Building onset can enter a lag bin. Target-level expected observed lag is defined as expected PV onset minus expected linked-Building onset. Onset is uniform within each cohort-label-implied interval, so its expectation is the interval midpoint. A left-censored PV onset is assigned to the first cohort upper bound. Records with a left-censored Building onset are excluded from lag estimation because their earlier Building age is unknown. These values should not be interpreted as exact installation-minus-construction lag. Negative lag conflict is excluded from the substantive annual curve. The right subplot is a 100% composition bar among resolved, non-conflict PV area: Building left-censored, cohort-contemporaneous, and between-cohort. The conflict exclusion keeps that QA pathway out of the three substantive classes. Unavailable Building onset is excluded. Status: `REPRODUCED`.
